Big & Small

A stone Golem and a tiny Sprite wake in the ruins of the Sunken Garden. Its fountain has run dry. Find the three rune stones, carry them home, and bring the water back. Neither of you can do it alone.

Needs a keyboard (or two gamepads) and a desktop browser. About 20 minutes. Press M any time to mute.

Before you set out

How to play

Room code
----

Goal: find the three rune stones hidden in the garden, have Golem carry them to the dry fountain at the far end, then lift Sprite up to the fountain's spout to wake it. Puzzles need both of you. If you fall into the ravine, you return to the last checkpoint.

Golem

  • Heavy. Pushes stone blocks and holds down heavy pressure plates.
  • Lifts and throws Sprite. Stand close and press interact to pick up. Tap again for a short, high lob. Hold to charge a long throw, then release.
  • Carries the rune stones. They glow in its chest.
  • Too big for small gaps. Can't jump while carrying.

Sprite

  • Tiny. Crawls through small gaps in walls.
  • Works mechanisms: levers, cranks, and nudging things off high places.
  • Hold jump while falling to glide. Can ride on Golem's head.
  • Too light for heavy plates, and can't cross the ravine alone.
Your current goal is always shown in the top-left corner, with the rune stones you have found.
