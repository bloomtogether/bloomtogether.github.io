Growing the garden…
A two-player garden adventure

Big & Small

A huge Giant and a tiny Sprite wake in a sunken garden whose fountain has run dry. Find the three rune stones, bring them home, and wake the water. Neither of you can do it alone.

Before you set out

How to play

Room code
----

Goal: find the three rune stones, have the Giant carry them to the dry fountain at the far end, then get the Sprite up onto the fountain's spout to wake it. Every puzzle needs both of you. Fall into the ravine and you're back at the last checkpoint.

Giant
  • Strong and heavy. Pushes stone blocks and holds down heavy plates.
  • Lifts and throws the Sprite. Walk close and press interact. Tap again for a short high lob, hold to charge a long throw.
  • Carries the rune stones.
  • Too big for small gaps. Can't jump while carrying.
Sprite
  • Tiny and quick. Crawls through the small glowing gaps in walls.
  • Works mechanisms: levers, cranks, and nudging things off high places.
  • Hold jump while falling to glide. Can ride on the Giant's head.
  • Too light for heavy plates, can't cross the ravine alone.
