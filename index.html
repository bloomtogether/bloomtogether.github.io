Before you set out

How to play

Room code
----

Goal: you start in the Everbloom Vale, around the sleeping Heart Tree. Walk through the glowing North Gate together to enter Chapter 1: The Sunken Garden: find the three rune stones, set them in the dry fountain and wake it. That opens Chapter 2: The Lantern Mines in the eastern hills, where it is dark and the Sprite's light leads the way, and then Chapter 3: The Windmill Heights, islands in the sky where the Sprite rides the wind, and Chapter 4: The Frostmere Lake, slippery ice, thin ice and frozen switches. Look for golden seeds, memory petals and secrets on the way. Every puzzle needs both of you.

Giant
  • Strong and heavy. Pushes stone blocks and holds down heavy plates.
  • Lifts and throws the Sprite. Walk close and press interact. Tap again for a short high lob, hold to charge a long throw.
  • Carries the rune and lantern stones. Pushes mine carts, winds the big winch, turns sails, wades through deep water, slides ice blocks and rolls snowballs.
  • Ground pound: jump, then press interact in mid-air.
  • Too big for small gaps. Can't jump while carrying.
Sprite
  • Tiny and quick. Crawls through the small glowing gaps in walls.
  • Works mechanisms: levers, cranks, and nudging things off high places.
  • Light pulse: press interact with nothing nearby. Lights lanterns and wakes crystal bridges in the dark.
  • Rides the wind: hold jump in a rising wind or a wind current.
  • Thaws ice: hold interact next to something frozen. Light enough for thin ice.
  • Hold jump while falling to glide. Can ride on the Giant's head.
  • Too light for heavy plates, can't cross the ravine alone.
